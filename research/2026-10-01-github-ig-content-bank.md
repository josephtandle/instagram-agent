# Instagram Content Bank: Trending GitHub Repos → Value-First Videos

**Prepared:** 2026-10-01 · **Owner:** Illy · **For:** Joe Che Instagram
**Direction it serves:** "Rework Instagram content strategy" (Joe's call feedback) — shift away from salesy posts to value-first video, CTA at the end, cinematic/inspirational saved for roughly monthly.

---

## The idea in one line

Joe's audience is entrepreneurs and operators who want AI to do their work, not coders. GitHub is where the real tools live. So each post takes one genuinely popular, real tool and shows what it does for a normal business owner in under 30 seconds. The tool is the value. The CTA is a comment keyword that sends them a plain-English guide. No pitch in the video.

This is a repeatable format, not a one-off. New repos trend every week, so the well does not run dry.

## Why this fits the new direction

- **Value-first:** every video teaches or shows a real tool. Zero "join my program" in the body.
- **Less salesy:** the ask is "comment a word, I will send you the how-to," not "book a call."
- **Cinematic saved for monthly:** this format is screen-recording + Joe talking to camera, cheap to produce daily. The polished cinematic piece stays a once-a-month event, exactly as Joe asked.
- **House rules applied:** problem-first hook (stranger-first, problem in line one), first-person voice, CTA is a first-person comment keyword, one post per day, no em dashes.

## The content bank (10 ready-to-shoot posts)

Each one: a problem-first hook, the 15 to 25 second value demo, and a comment-keyword CTA. Shoot as screen recording with Joe voiceover, or Joe to camera with the screen inset.

**1. Clone your own voice for free**
- Repo: VoiceStudio
- Hook: "You are paying for an AI voice tool you could run for free on your own laptop."
- Value: show a voice getting cloned locally, no subscription, no cloud.
- CTA: "Comment VOICE and I'll send you the free setup."

**2. The AI that uses your browser for you**
- Repo: browser-use / jev-ultrafast
- Hook: "I have not filled out a web form myself in weeks. My AI does it."
- Value: agent navigating a site, filling a form, pulling data live.
- CTA: "Comment BROWSER and I'll send you how it works."

**3. Clone a viral video in minutes**
- Repo: hypit
- Hook: "Someone's reel went viral. Here is how people are rebuilding the whole thing with AI in minutes."
- Value: show the face-swap, voice, full-workflow clone. (Ethics note: frame as "study what works," not "steal.")
- CTA: "Comment CLONE and I'll send you the breakdown."

**4. Give your AI a memory that never forgets**
- Repo: claude-mem / mempalace
- Hook: "The reason your AI keeps forgetting what you told it yesterday, and the fix."
- Value: show persistent memory across sessions.
- CTA: "Comment MEMORY and I'll send you the tool."

**5. Turn any website into data your AI can use**
- Repo: firecrawl / crawl4ai
- Hook: "Your competitor's whole website, turned into a spreadsheet, in one step."
- Value: website in, clean structured data out.
- CTA: "Comment DATA and I'll send you the how-to."

**6. A free AI office suite (Docs, Sheets, Slides)**
- Repo: genoffice
- Hook: "You are overpaying for office software. There is a free AI-native version."
- Value: generate a doc and a deck from a prompt.
- CTA: "Comment OFFICE and I'll send you the link."

**7. Run your phone with plain English**
- Repo: google/artemis
- Hook: "I told my phone what to do in one sentence and it did it."
- Value: natural-language Android automation on screen.
- CTA: "Comment PHONE and I'll send you the walkthrough."

**8. A CRM that runs itself**
- Repo: trycompai/crm
- Hook: "What if your CRM did the follow-up instead of just storing the contact?"
- Value: agentic CRM acting on its own.
- CTA: "Comment CRM and I'll send you the setup."

**9. Learn to build anything with AI (the free list)**
- Repo: build-your-own-x / freeCodeCamp
- Hook: "You do not need a course. You need this one free GitHub page."
- Value: scroll the list, pick one, show the path.
- CTA: "Comment LEARN and I'll send you where to start."

**10. The tool that strips AI watermarks from your own content**
- Repo: watermarks-remover
- Hook: "Your AI-made content is quietly watermarked. Here is how to clean what is yours."
- Value: before/after on a file. (MyOS already runs this internally.)
- CTA: "Comment CLEAN and I'll send you the tool."

## Cadence suggestion (fits "one post per day, never double up")

- 5 to 6 of these per week as the daily value post.
- 1 cinematic/inspirational piece per month (per Joe's direction).
- Rotate in a fresh repo each week from the refreshed pull, so the format stays current.

## How to refresh the data (self-improving)

Re-run before each planning batch:

```bash
curl -s "https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=created:%3E$(date -v-3m +%Y-%m-01)+agent+OR+llm+OR+claude&sort=stars&order=desc&per_page=20" -H "Accept: application/vnd.github+json" \
  | node -e 'let d="";process.stdin.on("data",c=>d+=c);process.stdin.on("end",()=>{JSON.parse(d).items.forEach((r,i)=>console.log(`${i+1}. ${r.full_name} ⭐${(r.stargazers_count/1000).toFixed(1)}k — ${r.description}`))})'
```

Screen each candidate on two questions before it becomes a post: (1) can a non-coder understand the payoff in one sentence? (2) does it demo visually in under 25 seconds? If no to either, skip it.
